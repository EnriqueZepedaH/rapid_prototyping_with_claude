The Neighborhoods

Chicago's Latin dance scene is spread across the city. Each neighborhood has its own personality.

South Loop
Home to Chicago's oldest Latin dance institution. Latin Street Music & Dancing has been here for 27+ years.
Logan Square
The family-owned Dance Academy of Salsa has called this neighborhood home since 2002.
Six Corners / Irving Park
Dennis PaSamba's studio is a Six Corners staple — 33+ years of dance, plus Friday night socials.
West Loop
Where the curriculum meets the dance floor. Latin Rhythms Academy classes lead right into Alhambra Palace Thursday socials.
Wrigleyville
Sunday night salsa at The Cubby Bear is a Chicago institution. Free lesson at 8:15pm.
River North
Havana Grill brings 1940s art-deco and live Latin bands to Friday and Saturday nights.
Rosemont
Once a year, the Midwest's biggest salsa event takes over the Westin O'Hare.

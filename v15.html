Find Your Neighborhood

From the South Loop to Rosemont, Latin dance lives across Chicago.

South Loop
Home to Latin Street Music & Dancing—the city's cornerstone for salsa, bachata, and cumbia.
Logan Square
Dance Academy of Salsa brings nightly classes in salsa and bachata to the northwest side.
Six Corners
Dennis PaSamba's studio offers bachata, kizomba, and Friday night socials.
West Loop
Alhambra Palace hosts Thursday salsa & bachata nights. Latin Rhythms Academy nearby.
Wrigleyville
Sunday salsa at The Cubby Bear—DJ Marz and social dancing steps from Wrigley Field.
River North
Havana Grill brings live Latin bands on Friday and Saturday nights downtown.
Rosemont
The Chicago International Salsa Congress draws thousands every February.

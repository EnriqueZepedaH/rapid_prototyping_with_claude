Explore by Neighborhood

From the South Loop to Rosemont, Latin dance lives across Chicago.

South Loop
Home to Latin Street Music & Dancing—the city's cornerstone for salsa, bachata, and cumbia classes and events.
Logan Square
Dance Academy of Salsa brings nightly classes in salsa and bachata to this vibrant northwest neighborhood.
Six Corners / Irving Park
Dennis PaSamba's studio offers bachata, kizomba, and Friday night socials on the northwest side.
West Loop
Alhambra Palace hosts Thursday salsa & bachata nights. Latin Rhythms Academy offers classes nearby.
Wrigleyville
Sunday salsa at The Cubby Bear—free lessons, DJ Marz, and social dancing steps from Wrigley Field.
River North
Havana Grill brings live Latin bands on Friday and Saturday nights in the heart of downtown nightlife.
Rosemont
The Chicago International Salsa Congress draws thousands every February for workshops, showcases, and social dancing.

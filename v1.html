Latin Dance Chicago

Where to learn and dance Latin rhythms in the city

Lessons

  • Latin Street Dancing
    Salsa & bachata classes — Tuesdays & Thursdays, Pilsen
  • Enamorados Dance Academy
    Beginner to advanced salsa — Wednesdays, Logan Square
  • Cumbia Collective
    Cumbia workshops — First Saturday of each month, Little Village
  • Ritmo Latino Studio
    Bachata & merengue — Mon/Wed/Fri, Humboldt Park
  • Chicago Salsa Congress Workshops
    Intensive weekend workshops — Quarterly, Downtown

Socials

  • Salsa Sundays at Havana Club
    Live band, open floor — Sundays 8pm, River North
  • Bachata Nights at Alegría
    DJ sets, social dancing — Fridays 9pm, Wicker Park
  • Cumbia en la Calle
    Outdoor summer social — Saturdays 6pm, Pilsen (June–Sept)
  • Latin Mix at The Midway
    Salsa, bachata, cumbia rotation — Thursdays 9pm, Logan Square
  • Rueda de Casino Meetup
    Group salsa rueda — Second Sunday of each month, Humboldt Park

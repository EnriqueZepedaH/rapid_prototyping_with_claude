Lessons

Step into the rhythm. Chicago's best Latin dance studios are ready to welcome you, from your very first step to the stage.

Salsa, Bachata & Cumbia
Latin Street Music & Dancing
600 W Roosevelt Rd, South Loop. Mon–Fri 6–10pm, Sat 12–5pm. Chicago's premier Latin dance studio for over 27 years. 80+ weekly classes, 10+ instructors, all levels. Free parking.
Salsa, Bachata & Merengue
Dance Academy of Salsa
2246 N Western Ave, Logan Square. Mon–Fri 5:30–10pm, Sat 1:30–5pm. Family-owned since 2002. Drop-in classes, 4-week courses ($110/person), and private lessons. All levels.
Salsa, Bachata, Cumbia & Kizomba
Dennis PaSamba Dance Company
4820 W Irving Park Rd, Six Corners. Evenings — check website for current times. Over 33 years of experience. Beginner-friendly group classes ($95/4 weeks, $15 drop-in). No partner needed. Free parking.
Salsa, Mambo, Bachata & Kizomba
Latin Rhythms Academy
West Loop. Mon–Fri evenings, Sat mornings. Chicago's leading curriculum-based Latin dance academy. 4-week sessions ($90). Salsa, mambo, cha cha, bachata, and kizomba.
All Latin Styles — Workshops & Performances
Chicago International Salsa Congress
Westin O'Hare, Rosemont. Annual, every February (25th anniversary was Feb 12–16, 2026). The Midwest's biggest salsa event. 60+ workshops over 3 days with world-class instructors. Evening showcases, concerts, and social dancing until 3am.

Socials

The classes are where you learn. The socials are where you live it. Join Chicago's vibrant Latin dance nights.

Live DJ Social
Salsa Sundays at The Cubby Bear
1059 W Addison St, Wrigleyville. Sundays 7pm–1am, free lesson at 8:15pm. DJ Marz spins salsa, bachata, mambo & cha-cha. Free dance lesson by Latin Rhythms at 8:15pm. $15 cover.
DJ Social with Lesson
Salsa & Bachata at Alhambra Palace
1240 W Randolph St, West Loop. Thursdays 8pm–1am, lesson at 8pm, social at 9pm. Stunning venue with salsa, bachata, cha cha & cumbia. DJs Matador & Jesus Castro. $10 before 9pm, $15 after.
Live Music & Dancing
Havana Grill Latin Nights
412 N Clark St, River North. Fridays & Saturdays, music starts 11:30pm. 1940s art-deco Cuban restaurant with live Latin bands on weekends. Mojitos, great food, and a packed dance floor. No cover charge.
Social with Lesson
Dennis PaSamba Friday Social
4820 W Irving Park Rd, Six Corners. Fridays, free lesson at 7pm, social dancing until 10:30pm. Salsa, bachata, cumbia & cha-cha. Free beginner lesson at 7pm. Just $10 entry.
Social Dancing
Mayambo Monthly Social
Location varies — check mayambo.com for details. Monthly events. Salsa-focused social nights hosted by one of Chicago's top dance companies. A great place to meet the community and practice your moves.

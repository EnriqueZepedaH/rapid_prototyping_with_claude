Getting Started

Everyone Starts Somewhere

✓ No Experience Needed

Every class starts from zero. The instructor teaches every step, every count. You'll be moving before you know it.

✓ No Partner Required

Most classes rotate partners. Come solo — you'll leave with friends and a whole new reason to go out on weeknights.

✓ Just Show Up

Comfortable clothes, smooth-soled shoes. That's all you need. No gear, no signup — just walk in and dance.

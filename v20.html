The Scene Has Grown

Every neighborhood has its own story. Some of these places were here when you left. Some are new.

South Loop
Latin Street Music & Dancing — still going strong after 27+ years. Some things never change.
Logan Square
Dance Academy of Salsa — family-run since 2002. A new generation of salseros.
Six Corners
Dennis PaSamba — 33+ years. One of Chicago's longest-running dance institutions.
West Loop
Latin Rhythms Academy + Alhambra Palace Thursdays. The neighborhood has become a dance hub.
Wrigleyville
Cubby Bear Sundays — still the Sunday night tradition.
River North
Havana Grill — 1940s art-deco and live bands, just like the old days.
Rosemont
The Salsa Congress — 25 years and counting.

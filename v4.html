Common Questions

Do I need a partner?
Nope! Most classes rotate partners, and at socials, it's totally normal to ask anyone to dance.
What should I wear?
Comfortable clothes and shoes you can move in. Avoid sticky rubber soles — smooth-soled shoes are best for turning.
I have no rhythm — is that okay?
Absolutely. Rhythm is learned, not born. A good class will teach you how to find the beat.
Will I look silly?
Everyone looks a little awkward at first — that's completely normal! The other students and instructors have all been there. You'll be surprised how quickly you pick things up.
How many classes before I can social dance?
Most people feel ready for a casual social after 4-6 classes. But honestly, some brave souls go after their very first lesson!

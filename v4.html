Welcome to Latin Dance in Chicago!

Never danced before? No problem. Chicago has an incredibly welcoming Latin dance community. Here's everything you need to get started — no experience required.

New to Latin dance? Start with a beginner class (look for the "Beginner Friendly" badge below). Most classes don't require a partner — just show up and have fun!

Lessons

These studios and classes are great places to learn. We've marked the ones that are especially welcoming to complete beginners.

Latin Street Dancing Beginner Friendly

Salsa & Bachata • Tuesdays & Thursdays • Pilsen

Their beginner class starts from absolute zero — no dance experience needed. The instructors break down every step slowly and the vibe is super encouraging.

Enamorados Dance Academy Beginner Friendly

Salsa • Wednesdays • Logan Square

A structured curriculum that takes you from your very first salsa step to confident social dancing. They have a dedicated beginner track every Wednesday.

Cumbia Collective

Cumbia • First Saturday monthly • Little Village

Fun monthly workshops. Cumbia is one of the easiest Latin dances to pick up — great if you want something less intimidating than salsa.

Ritmo Latino Studio

Bachata & Merengue • Mon/Wed/Fri • Humboldt Park

Bachata and merengue are very beginner-approachable dances. This studio has a clear level system so you always know where you fit.

Chicago Salsa Congress

Intensive Workshops • Quarterly • Downtown

Best for dancers with some experience, but they do offer beginner workshops during the congress weekends. A great way to get inspired!

Socials

Social dancing is where you practice what you've learned. Don't worry — everyone was a beginner once, and the community is friendly.

Salsa Sundays at Havana Club

Live Band • Sundays 8pm • River North

A lively atmosphere with a live band. It can be busy, but people are happy to dance with beginners. A great way to experience the energy of salsa.

Bachata Nights at Alegría Beginner Friendly

DJ Sets • Fridays 9pm • Wicker Park

The first hour is a mini-lesson before the social starts. Perfect if you want a little warm-up before hitting the floor.

Cumbia en la Calle Beginner Friendly

Outdoor • Saturdays 6pm • Pilsen (June–Sept)

Super casual outdoor vibes. No dress code, no pressure. Just show up and move. Great for dipping your toes in.

Latin Mix at The Midway

Multi-Genre • Thursdays 9pm • Logan Square

A fun mix of styles. The variety means you can try different dances in one night and see what clicks for you.

Rueda de Casino Meetup

Group Salsa • Second Sunday monthly • Humboldt Park

Rueda is group salsa in a circle — it's social by nature and everyone rotates partners. Low pressure, lots of laughs.

Common Questions

Do I need a partner?
Nope! Most classes rotate partners, and at socials, it's totally normal to ask anyone to dance.
What should I wear?
Comfortable clothes and shoes you can move in. Avoid sticky rubber soles — smooth-soled shoes are best for turning.
I have no rhythm — is that okay?
Absolutely. Rhythm is learned, not born. A good class will teach you how to find the beat.

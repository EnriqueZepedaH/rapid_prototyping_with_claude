What to Expect

☆

What if I have no experience?

Every class has beginners. Instructors break down every step. You'll be moving by the end of your first night. Find a first-timer class.

♥

Do I need a partner?

Nope. Most classes rotate partners, and at socials it's completely normal to dance solo or ask anyone.

◘

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and smooth-soled shoes. Skip the rubber soles — you'll need to turn.
